To update the firmware, just follow the same steps as the [Flashing the Firmware](https://github.com/zenaror/PicoAdapterGB/blob/main/doc/wiki/3-Flashing%20the%20firmware.md) section.

The only difference is that you probably already have a configuration saved. So, right after copy the UF2 file, the device will reboot and should load your saved configuration. You don't need to reconfigure everything.

It's already ready to use 😄 .
